# LA Insight AI

### AI-Powered Daily Crypto Intelligence

LA Insight AI is a lightweight crypto intelligence dashboard designed to transform daily market information into a simple, structured report.

The project focuses on **Data Analysis**, presenting market sentiment, risk level, top assets, market summary, and potential opportunities in an easy-to-understand interface.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo

https://la-insight-ai.onrender.com/

---

## 🧠 What LA Insight AI Does

LA Insight AI generates a daily-style crypto market intelligence report containing:

- Market Sentiment
- Risk Level
- Top Market Movers
- Market Score
- AI Market Summary
- Key Opportunities

The goal is to make crypto market information easier to understand through a clean and focused dashboard.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Market Intelligence
A simple overview of the current market environment.

### 📈 Market Score
A visual score designed to provide a quick snapshot of overall market conditions.

### 🪙 Top Assets
Highlights major crypto assets such as BTC, ETH and BNB.

### ⚡ Market Movers
Displays key assets that may deserve additional attention.

### 🧠 Market Summary
Provides a concise interpretation of the market environment.

### 🔎 Key Opportunities
Highlights potential areas of interest based on the generated market report.

---

## 🛠️ Technology

LA Insight AI is built with:

- Node.js
- TypeScript
- Express.js
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

The application is deployed as a web service using Render.
